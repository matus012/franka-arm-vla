franka-arm-vla: fine-tuning a small VLA to follow typed instructions (simulation)

A simulated Franka arm in Isaac Lab is told in plain English which cube to pick, where to put it, or what to stack it on. The policy is SmolVLA (~450 M parameters), fine-tuned on scripted-expert demonstrations one stage at a time, each stage judged once against a pass bar written before its run. Simulation only; no real robot.

Full demo, 4 min 52 s. For each skill: what the robot was told, the success rule quoted from the evaluator, the headline rate, the first 16 episodes from unseen demo scenes (not selected, real time, the evaluator's verdict on each), and one episode full size with what the policy itself sees. Download the mp4.
